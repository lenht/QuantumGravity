Chronology · 66 dated entries, six theories, one timeline

Every dated idea, in the order it actually happened

Each of the six theory pages ends with its own "Development notes" — the book's own date-stamped record of how that one idea grew, including the versions it later walked back. This page takes all six of those records apart and merges them back together by date, so you can see what the author was actually working on at any given point across more than two decades: a railway-platform sketch of consciousness in 2003 sitting right next to nothing else for years, then five theories developing in parallel by the early 2020s.

A note on what's below. Nothing here is new: every date and description already appears on the relevant theory page's own "Development notes" section, just reordered into one sequence and tagged by theory. A few entries span a date range in the book's own notes rather than a single day; those are placed here by the range's start date. Dates and claims are the book's own — several entries record ideas the book itself later abandoned or corrected, which is why some theories' colors reappear more than once in the same year.

The full chronology

66 dated entries, from a phase-space sketch at a railway station in 2003 to a freefall-odds correction in May 2026. Filter by theory, or read straight down to see how the six lines of work actually overlapped in time.

How this page was built. Every entry below is copied from the "Development notes" section already at the bottom of one of the six theory pages — nothing here is a new claim about the book. A small number of entries cover a date range rather than one day (for example "15 Jan – 5 Nov 2020"); those are sorted here by the first date in the range. One entry (Charge Mass Dualism's "Unitary rest surface," dated 1918 – 2018 ) is listed out of order in the book's own chapter — it's placed here in true date order instead, the same convention this site already uses elsewhere for the book's own out-of-sequence changelogs.
